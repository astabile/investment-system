# SYSTEM_RULES.md

## Purpose
This system exists to protect capital first and grow it second.
It is not designed to maximize returns, predict markets, or trade frequently.

The primary enemy is emotional decision-making.

---

## 1. Decision Frequency
- Portfolio changes are evaluated **once per week at most**.
- In practice, changes are expected to occur **monthly**.
- Running the script daily is allowed for monitoring only.
- No intra-week rebalancing is permitted.

---

## 2. Capital Injections
- New capital may be added monthly.
- Capital injections **do not change market regime**.
- New capital follows the current regime rules:
  - RISK_ON → invested according to allocation
  - NEUTRAL → partial investment only
  - RISK_OFF → remains as cash / parking
- No discretionary “buy the dip” actions.

---

## 3. Non-Action Rules
- If the system recommends RISK_OFF, no new positions are opened.
- If no assets meet selection criteria, the correct action is **to do nothing**.
- Not acting is considered a valid and correct outcome.

---

## 4. Exposure Discipline
- Maximum exposure is defined strictly by the regime:
  - RISK_ON → 70%
  - NEUTRAL → 40%
  - RISK_OFF → 15%
- Exposure limits are never overridden manually.
- Cash is a strategic position, not a failure.

---

## 5. Asset Universe
- The asset universe is intentionally small and stable.
- No more than 7 assets are allowed.
- Assets are reviewed **quarterly**, not ad-hoc.
- New assets may only be added if they clearly improve diversification.

---

## 6. System Modifications
- No rule changes are allowed during periods of drawdown.
- Any modification requires:
  - Calm market conditions
  - A written explanation
  - A minimum 30-day observation period before going live

---

## 7. What This System Will NOT Do
- No leverage
- No short selling
- No intraday trading
- No predictions or forecasts
- No optimization based on recent performance
- No emotional overrides

---

## 8. Definition of Success
Success is defined as:
- Ending the year with more capital than started
- Having followed the system without breaking rules
- Avoiding large drawdowns
- Maintaining psychological calm

Outperformance is optional.
Survival is mandatory.

---

## 9. Final Rule
If at any point I feel the urge to “just tweak something”,
I must step away and do nothing for 48 hours.

This rule overrides all others.

---

## 10. Cash and Cauciones
- Any capital not allocated to assets is placed in cauciones.
- Cauciones are treated as risk-free parking, not as investment assets.
- Cauciones do not participate in ranking, selection, or regime decisions.
- Yield from cauciones is a secondary benefit, not an optimization target.
- Idle cash is not allowed unless operationally unavoidable.

---

## 11. Monthly Capital Injection
- Capital injections occur at most once per month.
- New capital follows the current market regime without exception.
- The act of injecting capital does not trigger rebalancing by itself.
- No discretionary timing (“waiting for a better entry”) is allowed.

---

## 12. Rebalancing Discipline
- Rebalancing decisions are evaluated monthly at most.
- Daily or weekly signals are informational only.
- Positions are adjusted only if:
  - Market regime changes, or
  - An asset violates core rules (trend or volatility)

---

## 13. Drawdown Behavior
- During drawdowns, no system rules may be modified.
- Discomfort is not a signal.
- The system is designed to feel conservative during stress.
- Any urge to override rules during drawdowns invalidates the action.

---

## 14. System Scope Lock
- This system will not be expanded during the current year.
- No new indicators, assets, or strategies will be added.
- Improvements are limited to clarity and safety, not performance.

---

## 15. Closing Statement
This system is intentionally boring.
Boredom is a feature, not a flaw.

Consistency beats intelligence.
Discipline beats optimization.
Survival beats brilliance.
